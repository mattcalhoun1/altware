Different design goals

Blackout Comms vs Meshtastic

These are separate projects. Blackout Comms was built for a different interface, a different security model, and a different idea of what mesh should do.

OverviewLoRaMeshSecuritySignaturesOnboardingRangevs Meshtastic
  • Meant to be usable without knowing LoRa settings or encryption terminology.
  • Setup does not ask you to invent or type channel keys.
  • No app store and no phone in the path.
  • Asymmetric and symmetric encryption, ECDSA signatures, and frequency hopping are part of the protocol, not an optional extra.

If you already have a T-Deck, LilyGO pager, or another compatible device, firmware is on the download page.

Blackout CommsMeshtastic
MembershipIn-person onboarding by a root device. Chain of trust.Anyone with the channel password is in.
KeysGenerated on device. Private keys are not shown or exported.One symmetric channel key, entered by a person, often on a phone.
Direct messagesAsymmetric. Relays cannot read them.Primarily channel-level symmetric encryption.
RadioCluster hops. Direct packets stripe across frequencies.Sits on one frequency.
ClientEmbedded firmware. No phone OS.Phone app is the usual interface.
